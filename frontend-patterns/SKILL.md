# Front-end patterns — compatibility entrypoint

The maintained catalogue now lives in the handbook as
[Front-end Patterns](../dev-standards-handbook/topics/frontend-patterns.md).
There is one source of truth; the files beside this one are links for older briefs.

- **Building or reviewing code:** use the handbook router and the relevant chapter sections.
- **Reviewing the experience:** read only the assigned catalogue groups. Follow the user's browser-only scope; a pattern is a lead until supported by observed evidence.
- **Updating a pattern:** edit its catalogue entry in the handbook. Preserve its observed problem, proportionate alternatives, and a check someone can perform. Do not duplicate it here.

Existing references continue to work: fit, first-screen, controls, tasks, consistency,
wording, states, and accessibility each link to their corresponding catalogue group.
